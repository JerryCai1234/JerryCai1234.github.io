Automated Roller Blind Opener

Status In Progress
Tools Arduino Uno, Fusion 360
Category Mechatronics

A roller blind opener that automatically raises and lowers a blind based on a schedule and ambient light level — no manual pulling required. Built around an ELEGOO Uno R3 (Arduino-compatible) board and a 28BYJ-48 stepper motor.

How it works

  • A photoresistor reads ambient light level to decide when to trigger.
  • A DS3231 real-time clock module allows scheduled open/close times.
  • A 28BYJ-48 stepper motor drives a custom sprocket that engages the blind's 4.5 mm bead chain.
  • The sprocket is a custom Fusion 360 design (~32 mm diameter, 12 mm tall), 3D printed to fit the motor's 5 mm double-D shaft.

The hard part

The main engineering challenge was the mechanical coupling between the stepper motor and the bead chain: the motor has a double-D shaft, which means the sprocket needs a matching double-D bore (or a precise 4.9 mm press fit) rather than a simple round hole and glue. Getting that tolerance right in Fusion 360 before sending it out for printing was the key design step.

What's next

Finishing the electrical side with the RTC module, then testing the printed sprocket under load once it's ready.
